SCORING CONFIG — vendor criterion scores

Scale: 0.0–7.5, one decimal. 7.5 is the maximum; no 8/9/10.
Headroom above 7.5 is intentionally unused.
Neutral starting value: 5.0. Scores move up or down from there
on cited evidence only.

Bands:
7.5        category-defining; single best in market on this
           criterion; no material weakness
6.5–7.4    clear leader; ahead of category, gaps not
           threatening leadership
5.5–6.4    strong performer; above median, workable weaknesses
4.5–5.4    category median; baseline met; default when
           evidence is mixed or unremarkable
3.5–4.4    below median; real gaps, buyer friction
2.5–3.4    weak; material deficiency, customer pain or
           strategic neglect
1.5–2.4    severely deficient; broken, abandoned or eroding
0.5–1.4    effectively absent; does not credibly participate
0.0–0.4    non-existent or disqualified

Expected distribution per vendor (8 criteria):
1–2 above 5.5, 2–4 in 4.5–5.4, 2–4 below 4.5.
A scorecard with nothing below 4.5 is treated as inflated
and re-examined.

Evidence thresholds — upward:
above 6.5   3+ distinct evidence points, 2+ source tiers
above 7.0   multi-source and within 12 months
7.5         5+ points across both tiers, all within 12 months,
            no contradicting practitioner sentiment

Evidence thresholds — downward:
below 4.5   1+ specific gap or negative signal, either tier
below 3.5   1 Tier B source confirming practitioner pain, or
            1 Tier A signal of strategic neglect
below 2.5   2+ independent sources confirming material failure
below 1.5   multiple sources confirming absence or
            disqualification

Caps:
- 7.5 may be assigned to at most one vendor per criterion
  per market. Credible ties score 7.0–7.3 with a recorded
  tiebreaker.
- Where Tier A evidence is strong but Tier B practitioner
  sentiment is silent or negative, the score is capped at 5.5.
  This cap takes precedence over other criterion caps.
- Thin evidence in either direction resolves toward 5.0 with
  the evidence gap recorded.

Granularity: use the full 0.1 range. Avoid repeating the same
value across three or more criteria for one vendor.
