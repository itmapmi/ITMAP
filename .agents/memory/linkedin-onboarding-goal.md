---
name: LinkedIn campaign onboarding goal
description: The user's business goal for converting LinkedIn Lead Gen Form submissions into ITMAP users.
---

LinkedIn campaign visitors should submit a prefilled Lead Gen Form, have their information passed into ITMAP's database, and enter their account on their first authenticated site visit without completing another registration or password form. The user proposed a welcome email containing a magic link as the entry path.

**Why:** The user wants to run a LinkedIn campaign with minimal registration friction.

**How to apply:** Evaluate onboarding designs against this goal. Do not confuse automatic account registration with establishing an authenticated browser session.

**Confirmed landing destination:** The welcome-email link should open the advertised market's vendor map after signing the visitor in.

**Why:** The user selected this destination rather than a report or the home dashboard.

**How to apply:** Associate each campaign with its advertised market and preserve that destination through account claiming and sign-in.
