---
name: Markets AI chat grounding
description: Product boundary for AI-generated answers inside the mobile Markets research workspace.
---

AI Chat may generate answers, comparisons, and formatted tables on demand, but only from the selected market’s stored evidence. It must not imply web browsing, outside sources, uploads, buyer recommendations, or unsupported data.

**Why:** The Markets pages otherwise present fixed analyst evidence only. Chat is a deliberate interactive exception, not permission to synthesize new market facts or silently extend the available dataset.

**How to apply:** Keep the active market scope visible, disclose the allowed evidence types, cite stored evidence in answers, provide a clear out-of-scope state, preserve conversation continuity, and keep the mobile composer visible while the message thread scrolls internally.
